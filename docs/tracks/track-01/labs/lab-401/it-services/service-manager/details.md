# IT Asset Manager

## Overview

A specialist agent for tracking and managing IT and business assets in ServiceNow. Users can easily create, update, and view asset records, check asset types, model categories, and configuration items, all from one place. Ideal for managing assets throughout their lifecycle, this agent keeps data well-organized and accessible.

## Benefits

- **Comprehensive Asset Tracking:** Create, update, and view asset records with ease. Track hardware, software, and business assets throughout their entire lifecycle.
- **Asset Type Management:** Access and manage different asset types and model categories. Quickly identify and categorize assets based on organizational needs.
- **Configuration Item Visibility:** View and manage configuration items (CIs) to maintain accurate asset relationships and dependencies.
- **Centralized Asset Data:** All asset information accessible from one place. Eliminates the need to navigate multiple ServiceNow interfaces.
- **Lifecycle Management:** Monitor assets from acquisition to retirement. Track maintenance schedules, warranties, and depreciation.
- **Improved Data Organization:** Keep asset data well-organized and easily accessible. Maintain data integrity across the asset management system.
- **Simplified Asset Operations:** Perform asset management tasks through natural language interactions. Reduces complexity of asset tracking and updates.
- **Enhanced Visibility:** Gain clear insights into asset inventory and status. Make informed decisions about asset allocation and utilization.
- **Streamlined Workflows:** Automate routine asset management tasks. Reduce manual data entry and administrative overhead.
