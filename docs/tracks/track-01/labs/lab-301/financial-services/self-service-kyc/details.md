# Self-service KYC assistant

## Overview

In the financial services industry, when a user requests to open a bank account, Know Your Customer (KYC) needs to be done to identify customer risk. Front office and back office spend manual efforts on identifying the customer and creating customer risk profile and performing extended due diligence.

The Self-service KYC Assistant streamlines the customer onboarding process by automating identity verification, document validation, and risk assessment. This AI-powered solution enables customers to complete KYC requirements independently while ensuring regulatory compliance and reducing manual processing time.

## Benefits

- **Automated Processing:** Front office jobs such as application submission and identifying customer can be easily done by the agentic workflow.
- **Intelligent Risk Assessment:** Mid office jobs such as identity verification, customer due diligence, and extended due diligence can be handled by agent's reasoning.
- **Operational Efficiency:** Reduces manual effort in document verification and risk profiling. Minimizes processing time for customer onboarding.
- **Compliance & Accuracy:** Ensures consistent application of KYC regulations and policies. Reduces human error in risk assessment and documentation.
- **Enhanced Customer Experience:** Provides faster onboarding with self-service capabilities. Reduces wait times and improves customer satisfaction.
- **Scalability:** Easily handles high volumes of applications without proportional increase in staff. Adapts to different customer risk profiles and regulatory requirements.
